Selected technical and research projects

Computational Stability of Cubical Homology

Final-year BSc dissertation, University of Bristol · 2026 · Mark: 90%

I developed and tested a reproducible, parallelised Python pipeline for cubical persistent homology on 400 retinal images across ten perturbation protocols and 170 settings, separating spatial misalignment from sensor corruption for fault isolation in human-led diagnostic workflows.

View the public dissertation and implementation.

Cryptographic Implementation and Security Analysis

Vigenère–RSA with bigram encoding · Second-year BSc Python project, University of Bristol · 2024

Sovereign-Investment Safeguards Analysis

Foundation Certificate extended project, Kaplan International College London · 2023
