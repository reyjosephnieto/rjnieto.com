Profile

I am studying for an MSc in Science, Technology, Engineering and Public Policy at University College London (UCL). I hold a First Class Honours BSc in Mathematics from the University of Bristol and currently teach mathematics and information technology at Bulacan State University. My research interests are in computational algebraic topology, persistent homology, and topology-informed computation, with a particular interest in the stability of measurements derived from images. I am also undertaking structured independent study in dynamical systems, functional analysis, and measure theory and probability.

I also write Calculated, a column for the Manila Bulletin.

Before returning to formal study in mathematics, I worked for many years in public affairs, journalism, broadcasting, technical writing, and strategic communications, including roles in government, national media, and digital publishing.
